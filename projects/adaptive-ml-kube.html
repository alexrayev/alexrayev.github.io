Adaptive ML Kube: Universal Deployment Platform

A cloud-agnostic, write-once, deploy-anywhere Kubernetes platform for ML workloads, shipped to AWS EKS and to customers' air-gapped clusters.

Period2022 – present
My roleArchitect, builder
EmployerInfineon

Visit the live product →

The challenge

Deliver the same machine-learning platform to two very different environments: Infineon's own AWS EKS clusters and customers' on-premises clusters that are often air-gapped and managed by someone else.

What I built

  • Architected and deployed to production a cloud-agnostic platform following a write-once, deploy-anywhere approach.
  • Packaged it as a self-contained, cross-platform standalone installer written in Go, so a customer can deploy to an air-gapped cluster with one command.
  • Built the control plane with Argo Workflows, Helm and Kustomize, and Crossplane infrastructure-as-code.
  • Integrated Kubeflow, MLflow experiment tracking, the Istio service mesh and OAuth2 multi-tenant authentication.
  • Added Prometheus and Grafana observability for fault-tolerant, proactive operations.

Architecture

Outcomes

  • In production on AWS EKS and at customer on-premises sites.
  • One-command, offline-capable installation for customers.
  • Multi-tenant access with per-user isolation and monitored operations.
