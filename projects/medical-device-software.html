Medical-Device Software: Laser Lithotripsy and Remote Consultation

Desktop and client software for medical devices: a cross-platform laser lithotripsy system that shortened procedures from sixty to forty minutes, and a client that lets doctors consult remotely.

Period2017 – 2018
My roleSoftware project lead, client-facing technical lead
EmployerFull Spectrum Software Consulting

The challenge

Medical devices are regulated products, so their software has to be dependable, traceable and testable, and it has to fit the way clinicians actually work. Two consulting engagements put that into practice: a system used during a laser lithotripsy procedure, and a client for video consultations between doctors.

What I built

Laser lithotripsy system (for a medical-device manufacturer)

  • Designed and implemented a cross-platform system with a distributed producer-consumer architecture.
  • Built the desktop clients in C++ with Qt, QML and JavaScript; components exchange messages over ZeroMQ, and procedure records are stored in MongoDB.
  • Procedures became shorter: from sixty to forty minutes.

Remote-consultation client (for a medical video-streaming vendor)

  • Designed and developed the Angular GUI client of a video-audio, client-server streaming web product that lets doctors consult remotely.
  • Trained the people who use it.

Leading the work

  • As the client-facing technical lead, I coordinated technical and non-technical stakeholders and coached the team members.

Architecture

Message-based design

The system I designed splits every device into its own service and every screen into its own process, all connected by one message network. Devices publish their state, screens subscribe to it, and commands travel as request and reply. A last-value cache lets a screen that starts late show the current state immediately.
